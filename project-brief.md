# My project brief

## The question
Where are the gaps in borehole coverage in the Shebelle Transboundary Basin (Ethiopia-Somalia) relative to population?

## Data needed
- Basin boundary - https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrobasins - Zipped Shapefile for Africa - 523MB
- Population data for 2026 - https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/listing?id=135 -tif files Ethiopia 536MB & Somalia 16MB
- Waterpoint data Ethiopia 
- https://ihp-wins.unesco.org/en/dataset/ethiopia-groundwater-sources-from-mwater - CSV file 1253KB
- https://ihp-wins.unesco.org/en/dataset/ethiopia-wells-osm - CSV files 6KB
- https://data.humdata.org/dataset/eth_water_points - Shapefile 893 KB
- National Databases and Basin-Specific Studies
- Waterpoint data Somalia - https://spatial.faoswalim.org/catalogue/#/dataset/346 - Shapefile 1756KB
